# Cooking art direction

The approved concept images are preserved in dist/art/flavor.png, dist/art/pan.png, and dist/art/taste.png. The production environment plate is dist/art/kitchen-clean.png. The ingredient icons use CSS crops of the approved flavor image. All cooking controls and meters are live HTML; cookware and food are interactive Three.js models. The environment is an illustration, not a fully navigable 3D reconstruction of the concept art. Walking remains in the existing 3D market.

The clean environment plate was made with the built-in image generation tool, using the first approved concept as its edit reference. Final prompt:

> Edit this image into a clean portrait gameplay background asset, keeping its EXACT cozy low-poly pixel-textured visual style, warm amber lantern illumination, teal awnings, indigo canal market, original sage-green horned customer, and wooden counter. Remove ALL user interface completely: remove the speech bubble and all its lettering, all bottom panels and buttons, BUILD YOUR FLAVOR text, ingredient choice tiles, bars and labels. Reconstruct the market/counter that should be behind all interface. Remove the large central ceramic bowl and loose ingredients on the central cutting board, leaving a clear empty wooden work area in the CENTER and lower two-thirds where interactive 3D cookware will later be rendered. Keep the baskets of mushrooms, greens, chilies, butter, corn and garlic along the far left and right edges only as decorative props. Extend the wood counter naturally all the way to the bottom. Keep the friendly monster leaning on the counter in the upper left third, with the canal and lanterns behind. Entire lower half should be uninterrupted dark warm wooden counter surface, subtly textured, no objects in center. No text, numbers, HUD, UI, panels, logos, borders, or buttons anywhere. Maintain the same tall portrait aspect ratio. This is a clean game environment plate, not a redesigned concept.
